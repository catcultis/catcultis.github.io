Getting started

Your first steps in Catcul.

Create an account and sign in

Choose Create an account, complete the security challenge and follow the on-screen instructions. If you already have an account, use Sign in instead.

Forgot your password? Use the reset option on the sign-in screen. Never share your password or security codes with anyone.

Install on your device

Use Download App for the available installation instructions. On iPhone or iPad, open Catcul in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen. Availability and features vary by browser and operating system.

Chats & groups

Find your people and keep the conversation going.

Start a chat or group

Open Chats and choose the new-chat button. Find a friend or create a group, then invite members. Open a conversation and use its menu for shared tools and settings.

Your group’s room cat

In a group chat, open the chat menu → Group corner. Your room cat gains activity from approved saved text and audio messages and eligible completed multiplayer typing races. Deleted messages no longer count.

People who can post can choose unlocked shared outfits. Wave and spin are temporary effects for people connected to the room; they aren’t saved. /pet and /throwyarn send playful chat messages.

Check-ins and rituals

The optional daily check-in uses UTC. Answer to see current members’ answers. A check-in day counts toward the shared streak when at least two current members answer. Participation counts are visible before answering; old answers and membership changes may affect the streak.

Group rituals are optional. Owners or admins choose the idea; members can join, mark a day complete or leave. Changing the idea resets participation.

Guest rooms

A signed-in host can open a temporary guest room and share its invitation with people who don’t have accounts. The host can remove a guest or replace the invitation; guests can leave. Share links only with people you trust.

Guest voice is not available in this test build. Guest messages are server-stored, not end-to-end encrypted.

Games together

Open a group’s chat menu and choose a game; ask everyone to open the same game.

Most Likely To

Start a question in a group of 2–12. Each eligible member can vote once. Results appear after everyone votes, the starter reveals, or the round expires.

Imposter Cat

In a group of 3–12, join a lobby and deal roles. Most players receive a secret word; one Imposter does not. Give clues, vote, and follow the on-screen final-guess step. Keep your screen private.

Two Truths and a Lie

The starter writes three statements and privately marks the lie. Eligible members guess. The answer is revealed after everyone votes, the starter reveals, or the round expires.

Word Chain

In a group of 2–12, take turns entering an unused word beginning with the last letter of the previous word. You have 45 seconds per turn and three turns each. Players manually skip an expired turn. The game checks letters and repeats, not dictionary validity.

More chat games

Chat tools also include Typing Race, Room Trivia, Draw & Guess and Color Cards. Follow each game’s on-screen rules. Typing rankings and drawings are for fun, not verified competitions.

Word Bomb, Mafia and Drawing Telephone are not available in this test build. Game state and submitted text are server-stored, not end-to-end encrypted.

Calls & sharing

Connect with your friends in a way that works for you.

Start a voice or video call

Open a conversation and tap the phone or video button. Allow microphone or camera access when prompted. Calls depend on your browser, device permissions and connection.

Share your screen

During a supported call, choose screen sharing and select what to share. Not every device or browser supports it, and you can stop sharing from your call controls or browser.

Make it yours

A little color, a little character.

Culji and profile

Open your profile and choose Customize your Culji to change your character’s available appearance. The experimental admin 3D mesh studio saves a local draft, not a published avatar; it does not change members’ models.

Wallpapers and themes

Use the palette or theme controls to change the available chat appearance. Your own selected wallpapers remain yours; some themes and features may depend on your plan.

Photos and videos

Supported photos may be optimized before upload, which can reduce quality. The result depends on the source and device, and a smaller original cannot become genuine 4K detail. Video compression and a total-storage budget are not implemented in this test build.

Privacy & safety

A few things worth knowing before you share.

Who can see my messages?

Messages and shared game content are stored on Catcul’s server. Room access is restricted to authorized members, but these chats and Diary are not end-to-end encrypted. People who can see content may copy or screenshot it.

Invites and links

Treat invitation and signed media links like keys: anyone you give one to may be able to use it. Replace an invitation if it is shared unexpectedly. Removing a guest does not erase copies they already made.

Need a hand?

Quick checks when something doesn’t work.

The app or a game won’t load

Check your internet connection, close and reopen the app, and make sure you’re using the latest deployed version. Some group games require an active group and permission to post. Unfinished games can expire, and some turns need a player to resolve a timeout.

Microphone or camera isn’t working

Open Catcul over HTTPS, check your browser and device permissions, and make sure another app isn’t using the device. Different browsers support different call features.

Get more help

Use the Help Centre for more troubleshooting and support contact details. Do not send your password, reset codes or private invitation links when asking for help.
