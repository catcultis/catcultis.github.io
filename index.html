The little details

A little more clarity.
A little less guessing.

Honest answers about how Catcul works, what’s private, and how it fits into your everyday devices.

Who can join my conversations?+

Only people you connect with as friends or invite into a group or course room can read or send messages in that space.

Can I install Catcul on my laptop and phone?+

Yes — click Download App above to install Catcul directly to your Mac Dock, Windows taskbar, iPhone Home Screen, or Android launcher, with desktop incoming-call notifications and quick replies.

Do my messages disappear?+

Your conversations stay synced across your phone and laptop so you can pick up anytime, while unsent drafts stay strictly in your current tab’s memory.

What if I forget my password?+

Open Forgot password, enter your email or `@username`, and verify with either the recovery code or the one-click reset link from your inbox.
