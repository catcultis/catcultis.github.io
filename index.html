The little details

A little less guessing.

Simple should also mean straightforward. Here’s what to know before you make yourself at home.

Who can join my conversations?

Only signed-in room members can access a room. Someone joins by using an invitation, so share invites with people you trust. New members can read the room’s saved history.

Do my messages disappear?

No. Messages, photos, and voice notes are saved in Supabase so you can come back to them. You can remove your own messages, but downloaded copies, screenshots, and unexpired media links can’t be recalled.

Are conversations end-to-end encrypted?

No. Catcul uses encrypted transport and database access rules, but conversations aren’t end-to-end encrypted. Project administrators can access stored data. Media viewing links are temporary and can be used by anyone you share them with until they expire.

Do I need to install an app?

No app download needed. Catcul works in a modern browser on your phone or computer. You’ll need an internet connection and an account. Microphone and camera features depend on your browser’s permissions.
