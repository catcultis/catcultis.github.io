Privacy Policy

What we store to make Catcul work across your phone and laptop — and what stays strictly on your own device.

Effective Date: November 4, 2026

1. Information we store

  • Account profile: your email address, encrypted password hash, display name, @username, cat avatar settings, and chosen theme.
  • Messages & rooms: messages, reactions, polls, shared files/photos/voice notes, and call metadata in the rooms you join so your history syncs across your laptop and phone.
  • Personal Diary: entries saved in Diary Mode are synced privately to your account for convenience across devices (they are access-restricted to your login, not end-to-end encrypted).

2. What stays on your device

  • Unsent drafts: text you are typing in the message box lives in your current browser tab’s memory only and is never saved to disk or sent until you press Send.
  • 3D camera lenses & background blur: face landmark tracking and portrait blur run 100% locally in your browser using WebGL. Live camera video is never uploaded unless you capture a photo or clip and choose to send or post it.
  • Quick Notes (Alt+N): scratchpad notes stay in your local browser storage unless you copy them elsewhere.

3. Calls, notifications & optional location

  • Voice & video calls: live audio and video streams are transmitted over encrypted real-time media channels only while you are actively in a call.
  • Desktop & mobile push alerts: if you enable notifications, your browser registers an encrypted Web Push endpoint so Catcul can ring your device for incoming calls and deliver message replies. You can turn this off anytime in Settings.
  • Optional Nearby 500m & shared maps: Catcul never tracks your background location. Coordinates are checked only when you explicitly tap Nearby 500m or open a room’s shared map (which loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap).

4. Payments & third-party wallpaper gallery

  • Payments: card and payment details for Catcul+, Catcul++, or Catcul Premium are collected directly by Paddle (our Merchant of Record) and are never stored on Catcul servers.
  • Wallpapers: studio anime themes are bundled locally, and optional 4K anime gallery wallpapers load from Wallhaven (SFW only) when selected.

5. Your controls & deletion

We never sell your personal information or run third-party ad trackers inside your conversations. You can edit or delete messages you’ve sent, clear push subscriptions in Settings, export your Quick Notes, or request full account deletion through workspace support.
