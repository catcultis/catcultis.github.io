CATCUL — TOGETHER UPDATE (v26)

PUT THIS VERSION ON YOUR WEBSITE
Upload and overwrite the contents of catcul-web-only.zip on your static website host, preserving the folders. The connected database and call function have already been updated; the website files have NOT been uploaded for you. No Node.js or Python server is needed to run the site. Allow time for host/CDN caches and the Catcul service worker to refresh. If needed, close and reopen Catcul after updating.

WHAT'S NEW
• Draw together: open a private or group chat, then its chat menu and choose Draw together. Members share a saved board, with up to 600 strokes; any member can clear it. Strokes refresh by room signal with a five-second polling fallback. The public community room does not have a board.
• Send later: choose Send later in the chat menu to schedule a text from 1 minute to 30 days ahead. You can cancel pending texts, and failed texts appear for dismissal. Delivery is processed approximately once a minute and can be delayed by outages; permissions are checked again at delivery. Failed deliveries are not automatically retried. Scheduled texts are normal messages, not quiet messages.
• Send quietly: choose Send quietly in the chat menu. The saved message appears in chat and the unread count, but does not create a push notification and does not play an in-app notification on updated clients. Older open clients may still make a sound. Quiet send is text-only and does not include staged attachments or replies.
• Existing features include optional room presence, moods, room plans, Sparks and the signed-in Friends Games area. Catcul itself is ad-free. Third-party games can show their own ads; Eaglercraft slots require a suitable community-provided game build. The landing demo is local-only and is not the real messenger.
• Voice and video calls and 24-hour Stories remain free. Screen sharing remains a paid-tier control, but the P2P fallback is client-controlled and a modified client can present screen content as camera video; this restriction is not a foolproof security boundary.

PAYMENTS AND UNFINISHED EXTRAS
Paddle is in sandbox and its client token and price IDs are not configured. Checkout is disabled until configuration is complete. Gifting, bigger uploads, and any other proposed extras not present in the application have not been implemented and should not be advertised as available. Existing paid account entitlements are retained.
